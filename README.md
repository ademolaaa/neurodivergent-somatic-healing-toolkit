## 🧘 3 Clinical Exercises You Can Do Right Now

### 1. The Suboccipital Bilateral Eye Shift (60 Seconds)
- Lie flat on your back and interlace your fingers behind your skull.
- Keep your head still, pointing straight up toward the ceiling.
- Shift your eyes all the way to the right as far as comfortable for 30–60 seconds.
- Wait for the autonomic discharge: an involuntary deep sigh, swallow, or yawn.
- Repeat on the left side to reset the ventral vagal brake.

### 2. The Constructive Rest Psoas Decompression
- Lie on your back with knees bent at 90 degrees, feet flat on the floor shoulder-width apart.
- Place both hands gently on your lower abdomen.
- Allow the iliopsoas muscle to release chronic hip and lumbar tension without active stretching.

### 3. The 120-Second Low-Activation Task Initiation Framework
- Shrink the initial friction of any overwhelming task until your amygdala ceases resistance.
- Example: Instead of "write the 10-page report", the rule is "open the laptop and write 1 sentence."
